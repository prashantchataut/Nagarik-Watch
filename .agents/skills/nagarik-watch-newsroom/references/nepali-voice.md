# Nepali voice

Write natural newsroom Nepali, not translated product copy.

## Prefer

- short, direct sentences
- concrete verbs and nouns
- familiar newsroom terms used consistently
- Nepali numerals only where the publication style requires them
- specific empty-state language that explains what happened and what the reader can do

## Avoid

- generic phrases such as “महत्त्वपूर्ण भूमिका”, “समग्रमा”, “प्रभावकारी रूपमा”, and “यसले देखाउँछ” unless they convey necessary meaning
- literal translations of English interface language
- exaggerated civic slogans
- fake certainty, anonymous “experts”, invented quotes, and bureaucratic padding
- mixing overly formal Sanskritized language with casual product jargon

## Examples

Use “समाचार फेला परेन” instead of “तपाईंले खोज्नुभएको सामग्री हाल उपलब्ध हुन सकेन।”

Use “स्रोत खुलेको छैन” instead of “स्रोत सम्बन्धी जानकारी अद्यावधिक प्रक्रियामा रहेको छ।”

Use “सम्पादकले जाँच गरेपछि मात्र प्रकाशित हुन्छ” instead of “सम्पादकीय प्रमाणीकरण प्रक्रियापश्चात प्रकाशन गरिनेछ।”
