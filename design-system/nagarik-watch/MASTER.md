## Global quality gates (public)

- No side-stripe accents

- No em dashes in UI copy

- No glassmorphism as default chrome

- No identical card grids on index pages

- No eyebrow kicker on every section band

- Nepali + English pairs on recovery and error surfaces

- Option A: no membership / paywall chrome unless `NEXT_PUBLIC_MEMBERSHIP_PUBLIC=true`

## Homepage intent

Content-first edition front. One lead story dominates the first viewport. Secondary stack + latest rail support scanning. No SaaS hero metrics, no glass cards, no identical card grids.
