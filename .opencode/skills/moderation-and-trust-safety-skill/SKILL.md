# Moderation And Trust Safety

Do not launch open community features without a moderation queue, report flow, banned-word controls and audit logs.

Reader submissions require consent, evidence fields, optional anonymity and editor review before publication.

Fact-checking must preserve evidence fields and never fabricate sources.
