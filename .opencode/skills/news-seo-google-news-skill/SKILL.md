# News SEO And Google News

Before changing SEO code, verify visible trust signals: byline, date, author page, publisher, contact, editorial policy, corrections policy and ownership transparency.

Implement only schema that matches visible content. Use `NewsArticle` for article pages, `Organization` and `WebSite` globally, `BreadcrumbList` when breadcrumb UI is visible, and `ClaimReview` only when a fact-check page has an explicit claim, claimant, evidence and verdict.

Keep `/sitemap.xml`, `/news-sitemap.xml`, `/rss.xml` and `/robots.txt` working after route changes. News sitemap entries must be recent news URLs, not evergreen pages.

Never mark mock data or AI-generated drafts as verified news.
